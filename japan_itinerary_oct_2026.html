Osaka

Saturday, Oct 17 – Monday, Oct 19 · 2 nights
Saturday · October 17 · Arrival day

Namba, Dotonbori & Hozenji Yokocho

Arrival in Osaka: 2:50 PM · Easy evening

  • Check in, relax and freshen up.
  • Walk the neon-lit Dotonbori canal and see the famous Glico sign.
  • Dinner: okonomiyaki, takoyaki or an Osaka-style izakaya.
  • Finish with a stroll through Hozenji Yokocho, the atmospheric lantern-lit alley.
No packed schedule tonight—just enjoy the city after the flight.
Open Dotonbori on Google Maps ↗
Sunday · October 18

Osaka Castle, Shinsekai & Shinsaibashi

  • 9:00 AM: Osaka Castle Park and the castle exterior; go inside only if history really interests you.
  • 11:30 AM: Explore retro Shinsekai and have kushikatsu for lunch.
  • 2:00 PM: Browse Shinsaibashi, Amerikamura and Orange Street for stores, cafés and streetwear.
  • Evening: Dinner among the restaurants of Ura Namba.
Today's mix: one historic landmark, old-school Osaka, and plenty of shopping.
Open Shinsaibashi on Google Maps ↗
Monday · October 19 · Change cities

Kuromon Market → Downtown Kyoto

  • Morning: Breakfast and a final wander at Kuromon Market.
  • Afternoon: Settle into Kyoto, then explore Nishiki Market (if stalls are still open) and Teramachi shopping streets.
  • Evening: Walk the Kamo River and have dinner around Pontocho Alley.
A flexible transfer day. Nishiki is best visited before shops close in the late afternoon.
Open Pontocho on Google Maps ↗

Kyoto

Monday, Oct 19 – Thursday, Oct 22 · 3 nights
Tuesday · October 20

Kiyomizu-dera, Higashiyama & Gion

  • 8:00 AM: Kiyomizu-dera temple and sweeping hillside views.
  • 10:00 AM: Walk downhill through beautiful Sannenzaka and Ninenzaka.
  • Lunch: Eat in Higashiyama and stop for matcha, sweets or tea.
  • Afternoon: Visit the Yasaka Pagoda area, Kodai-ji surroundings and Yasaka Shrine.
  • Evening: Wander Gion and Hanamikoji; enjoy a traditional Kyoto dinner.
One of the trip's best days for charming streets and memorable photos.
Open Higashiyama on Google Maps ↗
Wednesday · October 21

Arashiyama & Saga-Toriimoto

  • 8:00 AM: Visit the Arashiyama Bamboo Grove early.
  • 9:30 AM: Walk through the gardens at Tenryu-ji.
  • 11:00 AM: Explore Togetsukyo Bridge and the riverside.
  • Lunch: Stay in Arashiyama for lunch.
  • Afternoon: Browse quiet, historic streets around Saga-Toriimoto, or return downtown for some free time.
  • Evening: Central Kyoto dinner.
Skip the extra temple-hopping today; take time for the scenery.
Open Arashiyama on Google Maps ↗
Thursday · October 22 · Change cities

Fushimi Inari → Tokyo

  • 7:30 AM: Explore Fushimi Inari and the iconic red torii gates.
  • 9:30 AM: Return for breakfast, packing and checkout.
  • Afternoon: Settle into your Tokyo hotel.
  • Evening: Keep it simple with dinner near the hotel. If convenient, explore Shinjuku and Omoide Yokocho.
The lower shrine trails are plenty. You don't need to hike to the summit.
Open Fushimi Inari on Google Maps ↗

Tokyo

Thursday, Oct 22 – Monday, Oct 26 · 4 nights
Friday · October 23 · West Tokyo

Meiji Jingu, Harajuku, Omotesando & Shibuya

  • 9:00 AM: Start with peaceful forest paths at Meiji Jingu.
  • 10:30 AM: Explore Harajuku and quirky Takeshita Street.
  • Lunch: Find a restaurant or café in Omotesando.
  • Afternoon: Explore Omotesando and Cat Street, then wander toward Shibuya's shops.
  • Late afternoon: Shibuya Sky for a panoramic view (advance ticket recommended).
  • Evening: Shibuya Crossing, neon streets and dinner.
Book Shibuya Sky early, especially if you want a sunset slot.
Open Shibuya on Google Maps ↗
Saturday · October 24 · Central Tokyo

Tsukiji, Ginza & Azabudai Hills

  • 9:00 AM: Breakfast and food browsing at Tsukiji Outer Market.
  • 11:00 AM: Ginza flagship shops, department stores and Japanese design.
  • Lunch: Ginza.
  • 2:00 PM: teamLab Borderless at Azabudai Hills (book tickets ahead).
  • Late afternoon: Explore Azabudai Hills or nearby Tokyo Tower views.
  • Evening: Dinner in Azabudai or Roppongi.
Tsukiji works better on Saturday than Sunday, when many market shops close.
Open Azabudai Hills on Google Maps ↗
Sunday · October 25 · East Tokyo

Asakusa, Ueno & Akihabara

  • 9:00 AM: Senso-ji Temple and the Nakamise shopping street.
  • 11:00 AM: Explore Asakusa's historic side streets and small shops.
  • Lunch: Tempura, soba or comfort food in Asakusa.
  • Afternoon: Explore Ameyoko in Ueno, then Akihabara for electronics, gaming and anime culture.
  • Evening: A final dinner back in your favorite Tokyo neighborhood.
Today's older, traditional streets are a fun contrast to Friday's modern Tokyo.
Open Asakusa on Google Maps ↗
Monday · October 26 · Departure

One last Tokyo morning

Flight departs Tokyo: 5:50 PM

  • Relaxed breakfast and coffee.
  • Last-minute gifts or a short neighborhood walk.
  • Checkout and leave plenty of time for the international flight.
Keep the day open. Airport timing depends on where you're staying and which airport you're departing from.
